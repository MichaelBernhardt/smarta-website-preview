# Competitor visual audit

Date: 23 August 2026

This short audit informed the field-led editorial refinement of the Smarta marketing site. It records the official product and company pages reviewed; temporary reference screenshots were not added to the repository because they belong to their respective companies.

## Useful patterns

- Lead with water infrastructure and the operating result. Show software later as evidence.
- Use real field or operator photography at a large scale.
- Explain monitoring and control in plain operational language.
- Establish trust with years in operation, installed sites, local support and real project work.
- Give customer sectors and case material room to breathe instead of presenting every idea as a card.
- Keep the primary enquiry route obvious throughout the page.

## Smarta decisions

- Replaced the hero dashboard composition with one real Smarta borehole installation.
- Removed floating telemetry, status chips, decorative circles and most rounded containers.
- Reworked sectors and capabilities as open editorial columns with simple rules.
- Kept the web and mobile screenshots in dedicated product-proof sections.
- Removed the generic numbered process section and numbered service list.
- Changed the installation carousel into a static body-of-work gallery.
- Restyled the contact area as direct contact information rather than a floating form-like card.
